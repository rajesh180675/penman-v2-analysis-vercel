## Why

The persistence prior the self-consistent model shows (`persistencePriors.generated.ts`, φ = 0.96 over 23 companies, as of 2026-10-11) is estimated on the app's own companies: today's large caps, selected because their returns persisted. This panel is chosen on what was known at its start and keeps every company for the years it filed.

## The panel

- **Universe:** every company that filed annual results for March 2023 on NSE in the industrial format, with revenue from operations of at least ₹500 Cr in that filing (consolidated where it files consolidated results). Left out at the start: 148 financialFlag, 0 lenderFormat, 3 otherFormat, 4 noFiling, 0 noBalanceSheet, 697 belowFloor, 5 lenderByLoans.
- **Followed:** 1007 companies; 2 whose later listings or filings could not be read are set aside (not counted as having stopped). 975 have core RNOA for at least one year, and each year's median is taken over all of them; 883 have two consecutive years and so add a year-pair.
- **Stopped filing before FY2026:** 35 of the 975 (merged, delisted or late); they stay in for the years they filed.
- **Measure:** core RNOA on opening NOA, from the filings as first reported (`asFiledRnoa.ts`). Results filings carry a balance sheet only from FY2023, so FY2023 is the earliest start and the panel's year-pairs are 2024→2025, 2025→2026.
