# Reversion of the core operating margin, survivorship-free

> Generated by `npm run accountability:margins` from `data/filings-panel/panel.json` and `data/filings-panel/history.json`. Do not edit by hand.

## Why

The forecast starts the core operating margin at the latest year's and fades it toward the median of the years before, closing a share 1 − α^h of the gap by year h. α is a sector template's constant, 0.84–0.92, set by hand. Until November 2026 a penalty for weak persistence (0.03–0.12) came off it, so the fade ran at 0.72–0.89; this panel is why it no longer does. On the app's own companies the margin forecast barely beats "nothing changes" (`docs/generated/frozen-forecast-scores.md`), and that sample is too small to fit a fade on. This panel measures the share directly.

## The sample

- **Universe:** the persistence panel's (`docs/generated/persistence-panel.md`): every company that filed March 2023 results on NSE in the industrial format with revenue of at least ₹500 Cr, chosen on that filing alone and followed through FY2026. 1005 companies were followed.
- **History:** each company's income statement for FY2018–FY2022, as first filed, where its listing holds one (60 companies have a listed year that could not be read). 87 years smaller than 1.5 times their own filing's quarter are left out, 68 of them FY2018, where some filings hold a quarter in the year's place; the rule also costs a few real years that are mostly one quarter (`fetch-panel-history.ts`). 355 companies filed all 5 of those years, 741 the last three.
- **Margin:** core operating income after tax over revenue from operations, on the rules a frozen forecast is scored on (`filedCoreIncome.ts`): revenue less expenses other than finance costs, taxed as the recast taxes it. Other income and exceptional items are left out, as the filing's own totals leave them out.
- **Forecast origins:** FY2023–FY2025. Each outcome is a year filed after the panel was chosen. A company that stopped filing has no outcome and drops out of that origin.
- **Target:** the median of the 5 years before the origin year, every one of them filed. The app's own target is the median of every year before the latest, 10–15 years for most of its companies; this panel cannot test a window that long.
- **Left out:** a forecast whose margin at the origin, at the outcome or in the target lies outside -50% to +60%, the forecast's own limits (16 of 1549 one year ahead).
